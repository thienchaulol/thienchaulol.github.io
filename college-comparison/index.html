Data & independence. This is an independent educational visualization using public, institution-level data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any listed college or source agency. These figures are historical and may not reflect current admissions policies.

How to read SAT ranges. SAT 25th–75th percentile ranges describe enrolled first-year students with reported SAT scores, not all applicants or admitted students. At test-optional schools, score submitters may be a subset. Comparing your SAT with a range is not a prediction of your individual admission chances or a guarantee.

SAT midpoint. Scores come from College Scorecard as separate Math and Reading percentiles. The composite here adds the Math and Reading 25th percentiles (and the 75th percentiles), so it is an approximation, not an official composite.

Axes. The vertical axis is logarithmic, which spreads out the most selective schools. The SAT axis starts at 1200 because none of these schools report scores below that. The scales stay the same in every year so bubbles can be compared as you switch. Where bubbles would overlap, they are nudged apart slightly, and a thin line points to each school's exact position.
