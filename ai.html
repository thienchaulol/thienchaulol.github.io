01 · Build

Developing coursework with AI

Everything students use starts here. AI does the drafting and layout, so my planning goes into how to teach it, not how to format it. The content and the sign-off are mine.

02

Collect

Homework comes in one door and gets checked for completion before it shows up anywhere, so class time goes to the work, not tracking it down.

03

Assess

Paper quizzes go from a scanned stack to feedback on each student’s page, so students see it sooner. Every paper is held to the same key: AI drafts the scores, and I decide them.

Results shape the next quiz. The question the class struggled with most decides where the next quiz gives students a second chance.

04

Report

Grades and comments come from the record, so each comment is built from what the student actually did. The final word is always mine.
